## The style

qlim8 makes carbon accounts for small and medium-sized Danish businesses. The photos should show the people who use it: a carpenter, a bookkeeper, a production manager, an auditor. They should look the way they do on an ordinary Tuesday, just in good light.

- **Authentic over perfect.** Natural daylight, light wooden surfaces, plants and relaxed workwear. Real places: a workshop, the office on a haulage yard, the edge of a production hall.
- **People in front of software.** qlim8 is on the screen, but the person is the subject. The screen never stands alone as a dense dashboard.
- **Calm.** People look calmly at the screen or talk to each other. No posed smiles at the camera, except in the portrait of Mads.
- **No suits and handshakes.** No ties, glass facades, high-fives or fingers pointing at a chart either.
- **Colours in clothing and props:** muted green, sage, navy, sand, oatmeal and warm grey. Avoid black, neon, strong red and large white surfaces. No black-and-white and no filter look.

### Green and blue

The pages are written for two kinds of reader, and the photos follow. This is about mood, not a colour filter. Blue never means cold or blue light.

- **Green pages** are warm and close to everyday life. Closer to the person, a little more clutter on the table, coffee and workwear. The reader should think "that looks like us". This covers the homepage, Construction and trades (Byggeri og håndværk), Transport and hauliers (Transport og vognmænd), Manufacturing and industry (Produktion og industri), the two hubs, About and Contact. Platform and overview (Platform og overblik) and Reporting (Rapportering) are mostly green with some blue.
- **Blue pages** are more structured and calm. A tidy table, papers in order, straight lines in the background, focused looks. The reader should think "this has been done properly". This covers Auditors and advisers (Revisorer og rådgivere), Audit trail and auditor (Audit trail og revisor), VSME report (VSME-rapport), Reduction targets and scenarios (Reduktionsmål og scenarier) and Supply chain (Leverandørkæde).

## Technical requirements

- **Format.** Heroes and theme cards in landscape 3:2, at least 2,400 px wide (2,400 × 1,600 px). Portraits in 4:5, at least 1,600 × 2,000 px.
- **Subject in the right third on heroes.** The photo sits to the right of the headline on desktop and below the text on mobile. Keep the left side calm (a wall, a table top, a window) so the headline stands free and the photo does not compete with it.
- **Room around the subject.** The pages crop differently on mobile, and the About page currently shows the portrait as a small square. Keep 10–15% room to every edge. Faces, hands and the screen must not sit at the edge.
- **File.** High-quality JPEG (90), sRGB colour space. Deliver each photo in two versions: a web-optimised file for upload, 2,400–3,000 px on the long side and under 8 MB, and the full-resolution original for the archive.
- **File names** follow the pattern `qlim8-<page>-<subject>.jpg`. Lower case, hyphens, æ as ae, ø as oe and å as aa. Extra takes of the same subject get `-2`, `-3`. Each photo's file name is in the list.
- **Post-processing.** Natural colours and a warm, neutral white balance. Light retouching, no heavy filters. The photos should match each other in light and colour so the site hangs together.
- **The screen** must be recognisable as qlim8, but its text does not need to be legible. The person is in focus.

Send us the model and screen resolution of the laptop, the tablet and any extra monitor at least a week before, so we can make them at the right size. How the screen looks best:
- The image full screen, no menu bar or dock.
- Notifications, wifi and auto-lock turned off.
- Brightness matched to the room, so the screen neither blows out nor looks dead.
- Angle the screen so the window does not reflect in it.
- If the screen is corrected in post, use only the same screenshot and keep natural reflections.

## Consent and rights

- **Model release** from everyone who can be recognised, signed before they leave. It must cover web, social media, print and presentations, with no time limit, and allow the image to be cropped and edited.
- **Location permission**, in writing, from whoever owns or controls the place.
- **No logos of real customers** in the picture: not on clothing, vans, trucks, pallets, sacks, cups, screens or papers. Tape them over or angle them away. Number plates must not be legible.
- **No real paper.** Invoices, binder spines and notices with real names and figures are removed from the frame.
- **People in the background** stay out of frame or sign a release too.
- **Usage rights from the photographer:** unlimited in time, for web, social media, print and presentations, cropped as well. Agree this in writing before the shoot.
- Releases and permissions are kept with the image files. They contain personal data and are stored accordingly.

### Suggested planning

The 9 photos before launch can be taken at six locations: a light office at a small business (1–4), a workshop (5), a haulage yard (6), a manufacturing business (7), an accounting office (8) and Mads' office (17).

Most of the others can be taken the same day at the same places: 9 at the workshop, 11 and 12 at the office, 13 and 14 at the accountant's, 15 and 16 at the manufacturer, 18 and 19 at Mads' office or a customer's. Only 10 needs a home office. That is cheaper than another shoot day. Change table, angle and people so two photos from the same place do not look alike.

Spread gender and age across the pages. Prefer real people from the trade to models. A carpenter looks like a carpenter.

## Checklist for the shoot day

Before:
- Screenshots S1–S9 and prints P1–P3 have arrived and are on the devices. Try them full screen.
- Model releases and location permissions are ready, one per person and one per place.
- Clothing: two or three sets per person in muted green, navy, sand and grey. No logos, no black, no narrow stripes or small checks.
- Props: coffee cups without logos, plants, notepads, pens and masking tape for logos.
- The light has been checked: soft daylight from the side and no direct sun on the screen. Choose the time of day accordingly.

On location:
- Tables are cleared of real paper, names and phones with notifications.
- The screen is full screen with notifications, wifi and auto-lock turned off.
- Every subject is checked for logos, number plates, client names and people in the background.
- Every subject is taken with extra room and in a few variants: closer, further away, with and without the screen in focus.
- For the heroes the subject is in the right third and the left side is calm.
- The portrait is taken in both jumpers, and the square crop is checked on the camera's screen.
- Everyone has signed before they leave.

After:
- Choose two or three takes per subject.
- Name the files after the list, and deliver both the web-optimised version and the original.
- Keep releases and permissions with the files.

## If you shoot it yourself

- A recent phone is good enough. Use the main camera (1x), not portrait mode, and the highest resolution. Crop to 3:2 or 4:5 afterwards.
- Daylight from the side. Never backlight from a window behind the person and never direct sun.
- Hold the phone at chest or eye height, not from above.
- Have someone else take your portrait. The eye contact gets better.
- Take many, pick few.

## Uploading the photos in /admin

1. Log in to app.qlim8.com as an administrator and go to Admin → Indhold (CMS) (Content) → Marketingtekster (Marketing copy).
2. Choose the page from the list, for example "Kundetyper: Byggeri og håndværk".
3. Find the field:
   - **Industry, theme and hub pages:** the "Hero" section, field "Billede" (Image).
   - **Homepage:** the "Billeder" (Images) tab, fields "Hero-billede" and "Tema 1-billede" to "Tema 3-billede".
   - **Contact portrait:** the "Om os" page, the "Billeder" tab, field "Grundlægger-billede" (Founder image).
4. Click "Vælg billede" (Choose image). The media library opens. Click "Upload nyt" (Upload new) and choose the file: PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 8 MB.
5. Select the image you just uploaded.
6. Enter the alt text from the list in "Billede alt-tekst" (Image alt text). On industry, theme and hub pages the field is right below the image. On the homepage it is on the "Indhold" (Content) tab under "Hero (øverste sektion)". The homepage theme cards use the card's heading as alt text.
7. Click "Udgiv" (Publish). On the homepage and the About page the images tab and the text tab are published separately. The change is on qlim8.com within a few minutes.
8. Check the page on desktop and mobile. If the crop is wrong, upload another take. "Brug standard" (Use default) removes the uploaded image again.
